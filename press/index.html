About Kyriacou

Boilerplate

Kyriacou is a privately held beauty and wellness company based in Poughkeepsie, New York. Founded in 2024 by Stavros Kyriacou, the company builds, owns and operates three houses with a team of more than 50: Kyriacou Cosmetics, a cosmetics brand and beauty exploration company; Omorfia Day Spa, a full-service salon and day spa; and OMO Med Spa, offering injectables, laser and clinical skin health under licensed clinical supervision. Each house is run as an independent business under one common standard. Learn more at kyriacouco.com.
