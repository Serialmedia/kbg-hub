Founder & team

Stavros Kyriacou

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Stavros Kyriacou is the founder and chief executive of Kyriacou. He owns and operates Omorfia Day Spa in Poughkeepsie, founded Kyriacou Cosmetics and OMO Med Spa, and oversees the day-to-day operations and leadership across all subsidiaries.

Kyriacou is owner-operated and privately held, based in the Hudson Valley, New York.
