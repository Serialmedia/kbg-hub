Stavros Kyriacou

Chief Executive Officer

Stavros Kyriacou is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Kyriacou Beauty Group and the operator of its portfolio of beauty and wellness businesses in New York's Hudson Valley. He is the owner and operator of Omorfia Day Spa in Poughkeepsie, the founder of Kyriacou Cosmetics, and the founder of OMO Med Spa.

Under his direction, the group develops and operates ventures across cosmetics, spa services, medical aesthetics, and incubation — each built, staffed, and run as an independent business under a common standard.
